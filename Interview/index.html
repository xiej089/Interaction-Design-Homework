MY MOM ON ICE

An interview with the woman I admire most.

WHO IS SHE?

My Mom

The person I interviewed is my mother. She was a competitive skater and won 25 world championships. To me, she is not only an accomplished athlete, but also the person I admire most and one of the biggest influences in my life. Her resilience, independence, and belief in creativity have deeply shaped who I am.

Me
When you think about skating now, what is the first thing you remember?
你现在回想起滑冰，第一个想到的是什么？
The first thing I remember is not the medals or the competitions. It is the cold. Winters in Northeast China were extremely cold. The wind could hurt your face, and sometimes my ears would swell from the cold. My hands and feet would go numb. But at that age, I don’t think I really thought of myself as suffering. I just knew that the next morning, I had to get up and train again.
我第一个想到的不是奖牌，也不是比赛，而是冷。东北的冬天真的很冷， 风吹在脸上会疼。有时候耳朵会冻得肿起来，手脚也没有什么知觉。 但那个时候好像没有觉得自己有多苦，只知道第二天还是要起来训练。
Mom
Me
What was the hardest moment during your years of training?
你训练的时候，最辛苦的一次是什么？
My skates often rubbed my feet raw. Once it became so bad that my feet were covered in bloody blisters, and the blood had dried into my socks. I had to slowly peel the socks away from my skin. It hurt, but the next day I still put my socks back on, pushed my feet into the skates, and continued training.

Looking back, it sounds almost cruel. But at the time, for athletes, it simply felt normal.
我的脚经常会被冰鞋磨破。有一次磨得特别严重，脚上全是血泡， 血和袜子都粘在了一起。脱袜子的时候要一点一点撕下来，很疼。可是第二天还是要把袜子穿回去，把脚塞进冰鞋里继续训练。

现在想起来会觉得很残忍，但那个时候所有运动员都觉得这是很正常的事情。
Mom
Me
You won so many championships. Did winning ever become “normal” to you?
你拿过那么多冠军，“赢”后来会不会变成一件很普通的事情？
No. I was still nervous before every competition.

Other people may see the championship, but athletes often remember the things they did wrong. The happiness after winning might last for a short time, and very quickly you begin thinking about the next competition. Later, I realized that the medals were not the most important things I kept. What really stayed with me was the kind of person those years of training made me.
不会。每一次比赛之前我还是会紧张。

可能别人看到的是冠军，但运动员自己记住的往往是那些没有做好的地方。 赢了以后高兴可能只有一小会儿，很快就会开始想下一场比赛。所以后来我慢慢发现，奖牌不是我最珍惜的东西。 真正留在我身上的，是那些年训练给我的性格。
Mom
Me
What did skating teach you about life?
滑冰教会了你什么？
It taught me that people can endure much more than they think they can.Sometimes you believe you have already reached your limit, but you can still go a little further. But as I got older, I also realized that persistence does not mean forcing yourself to do everything.

The important thing is knowing what is worth persisting for.
我觉得它让我知道，人可以比自己想象中坚持得更久。有时候你觉得自己已经到极限了，但其实还可以再往前一点。 不过我后来也明白，坚持并不代表一定要逼自己做所有事情。

真正重要的是，你要知道什么值得坚持。
Mom
Me
Do you think being an athlete influenced the way you raised me?
你觉得运动员的经历影响了你教育我的方式吗？
Yes, but maybe in the opposite way people would expect. Because I grew up under very strict training, I knew what it felt like for a child to constantly be measured against a standard. I didn’t want your life to have only one standard.

An athlete can be measured by seconds, rankings, and results. But a person’s life cannot be measured only in those ways.
有影响，但可能是反过来的。因为我小时候经历过非常严格的训练，所以我知道一个孩子被要求不断达到标准是什么感觉。我不希望你的人生也只有一种标准。

运动员需要按照秒数、名次、成绩被衡量， 但是一个人的人生不能只用这些东西衡量。
Mom
Me
When I was little, why did you always encourage me to draw things from my imagination instead of copying reality?
我小时候画画的时候，你为什么总是鼓励我画想象中的东西， 而不是照着现实画？
I remember when you used to draw televisions.If I had asked you to copy the television in our house, then all you would know was a rectangle. But I thought, why does a television have to be rectangular? Maybe twenty or thirty years later, televisions will be round. Maybe they won’t even have screens anymore.

I didn’t want to tell you too early what the world was “supposed” to look like. A child’s imagination is very precious. Adults have already seen too much reality. Children haven’t.
我记得你小时候画电视机。如果我让你照着家里的电视机画，那你看到的永远就是一个长方形。但我会想，为什么电视一定要是长方形？也许二十年、三十年以后电视就是圆的， 甚至根本就没有屏幕了。

我不想太早告诉你世界“应该是什么样子”。 孩子的想象力很珍贵。大人已经看过太多现实了，但孩子还没有。
Mom
Me
You always treated me more like an equal person than a child. Why?
你从小好像都把我当成一个平等的人，而不只是一个小孩。为什么？
Because a child is still a complete person.Being young does not mean you have no feelings or no judgment of your own. Of course, parents have more life experience, so we can guide and protect our children. But I never felt that “I’m your mother, so you have to listen to me” was a good enough reason.

I wanted you to know that you were allowed to have your own ideas, and that your ideas deserved to be taken seriously.
因为小孩也是一个完整的人。年龄小不代表没有感受，也不代表没有自己的判断。当然父母有更多生活经验，可以提醒孩子、保护孩子， 但我觉得“我是你妈妈，所以你必须听我的”并不是一个充分的理由。

我希望你知道，你可以有自己的想法，而且你的想法值得被认真听。
Mom
Me
Why didn’t you push me to become good at the subjects I was bad at?
为什么我不擅长理科的时候，你没有像很多家长一样逼我把它学好？
Because I have always believed that people are naturally imperfect. You were good at Chinese. You loved drawing and art. But science and math were not your strengths. That was simply part of who you were.

Why should every part of a person be cut into the same length?

The exam-oriented education system can sometimes ask very different children to fit into the same standard. But people are not the same.

Instead of spending all your energy turning your weaknesses into something average, I would rather help your strengths grow longer and longer.
因为我一直觉得，人本来就是不完美的。你语文很好，你喜欢画画、喜欢艺术，但是你的理科没有那么好。 这就是你。

为什么一定要把一个人的每一块都削成一样的长度？

中国的应试教育有时候会要求所有孩子进入同一个标准， 但每个人本来就不一样。

我觉得与其花很多力气把你的短板变得普通， 不如让你的长板学习得越来越长，越来越长。
Mom
Me
Were you ever scared that giving me too much freedom would make my life harder?
你有没有担心过，给我太多自由，反而会让我以后的人生更难？
Of course. It is impossible to be a mother and not worry.Sometimes I wondered whether you would have an easier life if I made more decisions for you.But eventually I realized that the wrong turns are also part of your life.

If I chose every road for you, maybe your life would be safer. But in the end, you might never know where you actually wanted to go.
当然会。做妈妈不可能完全不担心。有时候我也会想，如果我替你安排得更多， 你是不是会少走一点弯路。后来我觉得，弯路也是你的人生。

如果我把所有路都替你选好了，你可能会走得比较安全， 可是最后你也不知道自己真正想去哪里。
Mom
Me
What do you hope I keep from you?
你最希望我从你身上留下什么？
I hope it is not the championships, and not simply the word “strength.”I hope what you keep from me is the belief that you can always imagine another possibility. When everyone tells you something has to be a certain way, you can ask: why?Just like when you drew televisions as a child.It is okay if everyone else draws a rectangle.You can draw a circle.
我希望不是冠军，也不是“坚强”这两个字。我希望你记住的是，你永远可以想象另一种可能。当所有人告诉你事情只能这样的时候，你可以问一句：为什么？就像小时候画电视机一样。别人画长方形没有关系。你可以画圆的。

Me
Do you think you are a strong woman?
你觉得自己是一个坚强的女人吗？
I might have said yes when I was younger.Now I think strength does not mean never crying, never feeling tired, or never being afraid.Of course I have been afraid. I have been exhausted. I have wanted to give up.Maybe real strength is knowing that you can be vulnerable and still choosing to keep living and keep loving people.
我以前可能会说是。现在我觉得，“坚强”不是一直不哭、不累、不害怕。我当然也会害怕，也会觉得累，也会想放弃。可能真正的坚强，是你知道自己会脆弱， 但还是愿意继续生活、继续爱别人。
Mom
Me
If you could say one thing to the little girl you used to be, standing on the ice in Northeast China, what would you tell her?
如果你可以对曾经站在东北冰场上的那个小女孩说一句话， 你会说什么？
I would tell her:

You will not always be this cold.One day, you will leave this rink. You will have your own family, and you will have a daughter.There are many things you are experiencing now that you cannot understand yet.But one day, you will take the strength you gained here and give it to her in a gentler form.
我会告诉她：

你以后不会一直这么冷。有一天你会离开这个冰场，你会有自己的家庭， 会有一个女儿。你现在经历的很多事情，当时不会明白为什么。但是有一天，你会把你曾经得到的力量， 用一种更温柔的方式给她。
